About Requests by Colart

A Tailored Karaoke Song Request System, Hosted by Colart

Requests by Colart is a purpose-built karaoke and entertainment song-request platform — designed, branded and hosted end-to-end by Colart Digital Marketing Agency, so every guest request reaches the venue on WhatsApp in seconds, with no app to download.

Instant WhatsApp Requests

Guests open the venue's page, fill in their song, and it lands straight in the venue's WhatsApp — no extra app, no waiting at the booth.

Fully Branded Venue Pages

Every partner gets their own colors, logo and hero photo, so the request page feels like a natural extension of the venue — never a generic template.

Mobile-First, Zero Friction

Built mobile-first with fixed layouts and one-tap sends, guests can request a song from their phone in under 15 seconds.

More Requests, Better Nights

Removing friction from the request process means more guests take part, keeping the room interactive and the night moving.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Requests by Colart?

Requests by Colart is a karaoke and entertainment song-request hub built and hosted by Colart Digital Marketing Agency. Each partner venue gets a dedicated, branded page where guests submit song requests that are sent directly to the venue over WhatsApp.

Do guests need to download an app to request a song?

No. Guests open the venue's request page in any mobile browser, fill in their song and details, and send it straight to WhatsApp — no downloads, accounts or logins required.

Which venues use Requests by Colart?

Karaoke bars and entertainment venues across Lebanon, the GCC and beyond partner with Colart to host their song-request pages, including Tune Karaoke and Joe's Bar in Batroun.

Can Colart build a request page for my venue?

Yes. Colart Digital Marketing Agency designs, builds and hosts a tailored request page for any karaoke bar or entertainment venue — reach out via WhatsApp or email to get started.

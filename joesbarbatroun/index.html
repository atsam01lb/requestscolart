About Joe's Bar

20+ Years of Karaoke Nights

For over 20 years, Joe's Bar has been bringing people together through music, good times, and unforgettable nights.

Based in the heart of Batroun, Joe's Bar is more than a bar — it's a place where everyone gets a chance to take the mic, sing their favorite songs, and become part of the show.

With more than two decades of experience in entertainment and karaoke, Joe's Bar has taken its karaoke experience beyond Batroun, hosting unforgettable karaoke nights and events across Lebanon.

From a night out with friends to private celebrations and special events, we bring the energy, the music, and the microphone wherever the party is.
